## 1. Creative Concept
A complete departure from the "AI agency card template." The website is conceived as **Art-Directed Strategic Theater** — an unbroken, authored visual essay combining high-fashion editorial typography, contemporary brand exhibition spaces, cinematic framing, and authentic physical materiality.

The visitor does not browse a catalog of "cards"; they step into BRANDMANIA's way of seeing the world.

---

## 2. Visual Language & Banned Patterns

### 2.1 Abolished Patterns (Strictly Prohibited)
- ❌ **No repeated rounded rectangular cards**: We eliminate the "eyebrow / heading / paragraph / 3 cards / CTA" pattern.
- ❌ **No generic Bento grids**: No modular Apple-style feature boxes.
- ❌ **No pill labels everywhere**: Badges are eliminated in favor of clean typographic datum lines and editorial footnotes.
- ❌ **No purple glowing outlines on every element**: Purple is treated as directional light, ink, and raw identity pigment — not a repetitive 1px neon stroke.
- ❌ **No explanatory design copy**: No public-facing text explaining "15 PostScript paths" or "crumpled marble substrate." The work speaks directly.
- ❌ **No fake video player**: No deceptive play buttons or simulated media frames.

### 2.2 Replaced By: Physical Materiality + Editorial Space + Scale
- **Physical Material**: Authentic paper textures, folded creases, ink saturation.
- **Editorial Space**: Deep obsidian voids juxtaposed with monumental typographic assertions.
- **Scale Extremes**: 100px+ display serif headlines paired with 11px surgical annotations.
- **Architectural Brand Eye**: The Brand Eye is an architectural presence — framing sections, scaling up as an ambient vector, and anchoring visual transitions.
